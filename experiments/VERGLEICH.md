## Notes on validity

- **v1:** baseline (rubric with anchors 1/3/5)
- **v2:** v1 + four bundled changes: anchors 2/4, scale instruction, stricter compliance rule, two calibration examples
- **v2 tests four changes bundled together** (anchors 2/4, scale instruction, compliance rule, calibration examples). A difference from v1 can therefore **not be attributed to any single change**.
- Possible next step: **ablation** – leave out each change individually (v2 without examples, v2 without anchors 2/4, …) and measure again to see the contribution of each change.
- Small sample (few runs × 10 ideas): small differences, e.g. Spearman 0.90 vs. 0.93, may be chance.
- More spread alone is not a success – always read discrimination together with stability.
